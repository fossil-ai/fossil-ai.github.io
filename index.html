About

Deep learning that leaves the lab.

I'm a Senior ML Engineer at the Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory (APL), where I build, design, and deploy deep learning solutions for perception and robotics in the maritime and autonomous-systems domain.

My work centers on automatic target recognition — object detection and segmentation — across a wide range of sensing modalities: EO/IR, LWIR/MWIR, sonar, and hyperspectral imagery. I work end-to-end: training and data pipelines in Python (PyTorch / TensorFlow), and real-time integration and deployment in C++ with TensorRT.

Before APL, I was a machine-learning software engineer at Northrop Grumman, working with synthetic-aperture radar (SAR) imagery.

(The name: people often hear "Faisal" as "fossil" — so my code home became Fossil-AI.)
